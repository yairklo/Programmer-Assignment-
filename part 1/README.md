# Web3 Lab Rescue: Home Assignment

The team works on a research lab product with a frontend, backend, Solidity contract, and Web3 integration.

After returning from a holiday break, the team discovered that the code no longer works correctly. It is messy, confusing, and broken in several places.

Your mission: save the lab.

## Parts

1. `PART_1_MULTIPLE_CHOICE.md`
2. `PART_2_FIND_THE_BUG.md`
3. `PART_3_BUILD_UI.md`
4. `AI_USAGE.md`

## Submission

Submit:

- Your answers to Part 1
- Your code changes for Part 2
- Your implementation for Part 3
- A short explanation of what you fixed and why
- Completed `AI_USAGE.md`

Prefer clear, minimal fixes over a full rewrite.
